OUR MISSION

F3 is a peer-led, zero-cost fitness movement for men that invigorates male community leadership.

PLANT

We plant small workout groups in communities across the nation, creating spaces for men to gather, sweat, and grow together.

GROW

Through fitness, fellowship, and faith, we help men grow stronger physically, build deeper relationships, and develop character.

SERVE

We serve our communities by developing leaders who give back, creating positive change in neighborhoods and families.
